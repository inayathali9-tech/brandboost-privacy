Privacy Policy

Brand Boost Studio, Hyderabad, India. Effective date: 16 October 2026

Brand Boost Studio ("we", "us") helps local businesses grow online. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you chat with us on WhatsApp, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you message our WhatsApp number, we receive and store:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the messages you send us.
  • If you ask to book a service: your name, your shop or business name, your area in Hyderabad, the service you chose, and the contact number you give us.

We do not ask for payment card details, bank details, passwords or government ID numbers in the chat, and you should not send them.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and show you our services and prices.
  • To review your request and confirm or decline it.
  • To contact you about the service you asked for, including the payment and delivery steps.

Our WhatsApp assistant follows a fixed set of replies. Your request is reviewed by a person (the owner) before anything is confirmed to you. We do not use your information for automated decisions about you, and we do not send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

3. Who can see your information

  • Brand Boost Studio: the owner receives your request details on WhatsApp.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: messages are carried through the WhatsApp Business Platform, which is run by Meta Platforms. Meta handles your messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting provider: our chat assistant runs on a cloud hosting service (Render) that stores the data described below on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Unfinished chats (a booking you started but did not complete) are deleted after about 2 days.
  • Requests that have been approved or declined are deleted after about 90 days.
  • Requests still waiting for review are kept until they are decided.

Messages may also remain in WhatsApp itself, on your phone and on ours, until deleted there.

5. How we protect it

Requests to our system are verified as coming from WhatsApp, the stored data is not published on any web page, access keys are kept as protected settings rather than in our code, and our logs do not store names, message text or full phone numbers. No online system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

6. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also stop the conversation at any time by sending the word CANCEL, or block our number. To make a request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.
